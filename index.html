Who We Are

Bridging Environment
& Development

Plus Egypt is a pioneering consultancy firm dedicated to fostering sustainable development and environmental conservation.

Established with a vision to bridge the gap between economic growth and ecological preservation, we offer a comprehensive portfolio of services designed to address complex challenges at the intersection of environment, economy, and society.

Our Vision

To be a leading catalyst for sustainable development in Egypt and the region, empowering communities and organizations to thrive in harmony with nature.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, science-based, and economically viable solutions that promote environmental conservation and integrate the value of natural capital into decision-making.

🌿

Environmental Conservation

Protecting ecosystems and conserving biodiversity across Egypt and the MENA region.

📊

Natural Capital Accounting

Transforming nature from a passive backdrop into a strategic, measurable asset.

🤝

Community Development

Empowering local communities and enhancing livelihoods across socioeconomic dimensions.
